# 📖 Wesley's Reading Adventure

A learn-to-read app for a 5-year-old, made for iPad (landscape or portrait) and phones. It's plain HTML/CSS/JS with no build step, and it works offline once loaded.

**Play:** https://nptrulock.github.io/wesleys-reading/ (on iPad: Share → Add to Home Screen)

## Modes (v3 expansion)
- **🔤 Letter Rain — 64 levels** in a phonics scope & sequence: letters 1–4 → big & little letters → first sounds → short a/i/o/u/e + mixed → word families (at/an/ap/ag, ig/in/it/ip, op/ot/og, ug/un/en/et) → sight words (10 sets, pre-primer to 3rd grade) → digraphs (sh, ch/th, ck/wh/ng) → l/r/s blends, end blends, double letters → magic e (a_e, i_e, o_e/u_e) → vowel teams (ai/ay, ee/ea, oa, igh, y-as-i, oo) → r-controlled (ar, or, er/ir/ur) → diphthongs (ou/ow, oi/oy, aw/au) → plurals, -ing, -ed → compound words → 2-syllable → themes (castles, dragons & magic, animals, space, building, food) → 3-syllable → giant words. ~970 distinct words.
  - Each round is one of 9 **tries** that rotate per level: spell, missing letter, two missing letters, first / last / middle sound, rhyme (catch a word that rhymes), word hunt (hear it, catch the falling word), find the letter, big-and-little letter match. Vowel teams and r-controlled sounds fall as single tiles (ai, igh, ar, ou…) with their own recorded sounds.
  - Difficulty climbs with the level number: more tiles (6→10), faster fall, more look-alike distractors, the answer arrives later, letter guides disappear from level 29 on.
  - **Slow mastery:** clean answer = 1 point, one slip = 0.6, more = 0.3. Each level has a goal (8 → 30 points) and ★ / ★★ / ★★★ at a third, two thirds and the goal. From level 9 the goal also needs practice on 2 different days (3 from level 37). Each round mixes in one review word from an earlier level. The next level opens at ★★★ (or from the parent area).
- **📖 Story Time:** 33 stories on 8 shelves (knights, dragons, pirates, dinosaurs, space, building, farm, weather…). Read 3 stories on a shelf to open the next one. Tap any word to hear it; 🔊 reads the page with word highlighting.
- **🏎️ Word Race — 12 levels:** "Pigs jump." → "I see the pig." → "Can the pig jump?" → "The pig and the cat jump." → "The pig is jumping in the mud." → "Look! The big pig is jumping." → "The pig hops and spins on a log." → "The big pig and the little cat dance on the moon." 42 animals/things, 30 verbs (+ -s and -ing), 26 adjectives, 24 places. Each level: 6 → 28 sentences, multi-day from level 5.
- **🤸 Action Words — 8 levels:** 38 action words; Read & Do phrases are generated from templates ("W and W", "the dragon can W and W", "W, W, then W"…), so there are thousands of combos. Each action plays fully, one after the other. Match / Play / Mix as before; tap the level chip for the level map.
- **⏭️ Next** is always visible in every mode. The round card shows the level's stars and progress bar; "Next level" appears once the next level is open.
- **🏰 Star Gallery — 18 rooms, 362 items:** My Room, Castle, Dragon Cave, Build Shop, Jungle, Ocean, Space, Farm, Dino Valley, Pirate Ship, Wizard Tower, Knight Yard, Snow Land, Bakery, City Street, Garden, Treasure Vault, Dragon Mountain. Tapping an item says its name, shows the word under it and makes it act (dragon breathes fire, rocket launches, fish swims, lamp lights up, cars drive…); the 2nd tap adds a recorded fun line; the 3rd tap plays an interaction with a friend in the room (52 of them: cat chases mouse, knight + dragon, monkey eats banana, rocket flies to the moon…). Room levels every 5 items, 5 decorations per room (lights, balloons, rainbow, night sky, party), a 🎯 next-goal chip, and each new room opens after 8 items in the room before it (5 for the first 7 rooms).
- **Dialog:** 700+ recorded lines — 60 praise lines, 30 encouragements, hints per round type, round/level-up lines, Sparky the dragon, room welcomes, a fun line per gallery item and per interaction. Lines never repeat back-to-back.

## v4/v5: new modes, medals, Word Galleries
- **⭐ 10-star levels, bronze → silver → gold (no day locks anywhere):** each round gives 1–3 level stars (3 if ≥80% clean, 2 if ≥50%, otherwise 1). 10 stars = 🥉 bronze, and the next level opens immediately. The level then keeps going to 20 = 🥈 silver and 30 = 🥇 gold. Every level card and level map shows three medal bars and "7 of 10 🥉", and the voice says "3 more stars to the next level / silver / gold medal!". Replay is unlimited. Next and the parent unlock stay.
- **🎖️ Word Galleries:** each medal unlocks the next reward on a 96-item track (gold gives 2). There are 60 **secret words** (kaboom, tyrannosaurus, abracadabra…) that say themselves and act out, 30 **stickers** (KAPOW, ZOOM, ROAR…), and 6 **Fancy Letters** that restyle words across the whole game: Bubble, Chalk, Castle, Dragon Fire, Robot and Space.
- **🪄 Vanishing — 24 levels:** fill in the phrase by picking words from 2–4 tiles. Help fades away in order: 🔊 the word read aloud, then 🖼️ its picture, then 🔤 its letters (partly), then no help. A magic wand "poofs" each support away. A miss brings help back for that space. Clean rounds step down a support and rough rounds step one back up. Phrases grow from 2 words to long sentences, mostly action phrases.
- **🔍 Picture Match — 20 levels:** hear or read a clue and tap the picture it describes. It starts with spoken 3-fact riddles and 2 pictures. Later the clues get fewer words and the look-alikes differ by one detail (color, size, number, action, place). Then come read-only clues and finally multi-detail sentences ("A little orange monkey is jumping on a log"). The pictures move: they sleep, jump, spin, dance, swim and fly.
- **🤸 Action Words — 20-level ladder:** Watch & Say → Tap the Action → Read It → Read & Do (2, then 3, then 4 moves) → Read the Sentence, each with a goal and a spoken instruction. **Mix** (the old free play) becomes a reward that opens at level 7.
- **Action payoffs:** every right answer sets off a star explosion with a boom/zing/crash sound and a cheering friend. The word flies into its space and the picture acts out the verb. Combos replay, and medals spin with confetti.
- Word-car letters sit fully above the wheels. The newer gallery rooms use an even grid with no overlap. About 270 more word pictures.
- Saves migrate automatically: points become 10-star progress, levels that were only held back by a day lock open, and existing progress counts toward bronze.

## Audio
Every word, letter name, letter sound, instruction, praise line and story page is a pre-recorded MP3 (`audio/`, edge-tts `en-US-AvaNeural`, made by `tools/gen_audio.py`).
- **Playback:** "Tap to start" resumes Web Audio (and sets `navigator.audioSession.type='playback'`). Clips are fetched and decoded into buffers once, preloaded for what's on screen, and scheduled back-to-back, so sentences play with no gaps. If Web Audio isn't running, or the parent area's "Voice player" is set to HTML audio, it uses a shared `<audio>` element instead. That path never waits more than 2.5 s for a clip that won't start. `speechSynthesis` is the last resort.
- **Caching (v3):** the service worker precaches only the app shell (14 files). Voice clips are cached as they are played/warmed (each level, room and story warms its own clips) in a separate `wes-audio-1` cache that survives app updates; clips cached by v1/v2 are carried over. The parent area has "Save all sounds for offline" (2,440 clips, ~21 MB). Bump `AUDIO_CACHE` in `tools/build_sw.py` only if existing clips are regenerated under the same names.
- **Service worker:** answers Safari's byte-`Range` requests with 206 partial responses. v1 returned the full cached file, which makes iOS media playback fail or stall. The install step fetches with `cache: 'reload'` so an update never re-caches stale files. A new build reloads the start screen automatically, or shows "New version — tap to update" if he's mid-game.
- **Trim:** v2 uses an energy-based trim. v1's ffmpeg `silenceremove` turned `cats.mp3` into 0.1 s of silence. `tools/test.py` decodes every clip the app can say.

## Grown-ups
Press and hold the faint ⚙️ (bottom right of home) for about 2 seconds, then answer a times-table question. From there you can see progress and the trickiest words, set levels, switch to capital letters, run a sound check, give stars, or reset.

## Dev
- Content lives in `tools/content/*.py` (levels, stories.txt, rooms, race/action vocab, dialog, emoji). `python3 tools/content/build.py` writes `content.js`, which the app merges over `data.js`.
- `python3 tools/gen_audio.py`: generates missing clips and `audio/manifest.js` (reads data.js + content.js).
- `python3 tools/audit_audio.py`: decodes every clip and checks duration, peak, RMS and an over-trim heuristic.
- `python3 tools/build_sw.py`: rebuilds the `sw.js` precache list and version. Run it after any change.
- `python3 tools/test3.py [url] [size]`: v3–v5 checks, including Vanishing, Picture Match, the Action ladder, medals, Word Galleries, fonts, word-car clearance and the gallery grid; plus (all round types, mastery + multi-day rule, shelves, long race levels, gallery actions/interactions/decor, v2-save migration, full clip decode, lazy audio cache, offline).
- `python3 tools/test.py [url]` (v2 checks): headless Chrome test at 1024×768, 820×1180 and 390×844. It covers every mode, console errors, that all clips are reachable, play() resolving, and offline mode.
